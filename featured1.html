INCOMING TRANSMISSION: "New CCDEVAP Student Logged in Orbit

When I was a kid I really wanted to become an astronaut, I wanted that in this lifetime of mine as I am myself right now I would see the Earth in a view where only 0.001% of the world's population have seen. I guess I just wanted to be so different before, but I started wanting to become an astronaut when I was in grade 6. In grade 7 I met a girl who was my classmate and we instantly clicked because of our love for space, then she said she also wanted to become an astronaut so every lunch time behind the chairs, infront of the aircon we would act like we were on a space mission and all our classmates know that we were annoyingly inlove with space though sometimes they would join our space missions as well. That is also the reason why I took up STEM, after the pandemic all my ambitions and interests changed and my love for space has expired or more like archived. But it was like intuition or second nature for me to choose STEM because of my years of learning about space, tech, rockets and what not. I thought of being a pilot but I didnt get to apply for pilot school before college, I didnt even apply for DLSU. But DLSU extended their application and so why not, this is the closest I can get to my dreams and even though reality hit me and I might not go to space, I'd still want to try or come close to it.
