Featured Stories: What Kyla Loves Doing the Most

My most happiest place on earth is at a dance studio with music blasting from the speakers while moving my body. I don't know what it is but I really am the happiest whenever I'm dancing. I really enjoy music, I am so so so soooo fond of it. I actually also waanted to become a music producer before, that was my second choice if I didnt get into DLSU SHS STEM. Sadly I dont do it often now in college, every summer i try to take more classes, but not like before where I would take atleast twice a month. I feel passionate about it. Like a burning desire to do it. So my most proudest work in the past years I have stayed in DLSU, I thin its when I joined DLSU Animove Dance Group and I got to plan a production, train with amazing people, learn from different choreographers who are also my peers, and just share the same mirror with these amazing dancers. All my friends and family went, and I was so happy to share all our hard work to them. I also got to perform in the DLSU Homecoming because the Animove Vietnam Team won Gold Medal. I am so proud of the people in the team and I met and connected with so much amazing people in the dance community which are now my bestest friends. I really love them so much, and I love dance so much!
